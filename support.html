Quick answers for following teams, sharing matchups and understanding score updates.

Why is a lock-screen score behind?

There is no developer score-push service. iOS decides when the app can refresh in the background. Open Standings Local and refresh the match for another provider check. The latest provider report may itself be delayed. Force-quitting, Background App Refresh settings and network conditions can prevent background updates.

How do I share an upcoming matchup?

Open Match Center → Statistics → Game-day comparison and tap the share icon. The image includes both teams and the available comparison data. Choose statistics in Share Studio to remove unwanted rows. Supported hockey leagues can calculate a broader sample using Calculate available season stats.

How do I browse widget teams?

Create a Teams design in Widget studio and select teams across your enabled leagues. Add the widget from the iOS gallery and select the saved design. Use page arrows to change teams and More games for additional schedule rows. iOS widgets do not support free scrolling or custom swipe gestures.

Why is a player photo, roster or statistic missing?

Coverage differs by provider, competition and game. Missing metrics are not invented. Include the league, team/player, date and app version when reporting an issue.

How can I back up my data?

Settings → Your data, your control offers export and import. Keep exported files private: they contain your preferences and selections. iCloud preference sync also uses your device’s Apple account where available.
