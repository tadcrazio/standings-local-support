How Standings Local handles app data, public-feed requests and your privacy choices.

No Standings account or score backend

Standings Local does not require a Standings account and has no developer-operated score server. The app does not include a developer advertising or analytics SDK. This does not mean that external providers or Apple receive no information.

Data stored on your device

The app stores followed teams, tracked players, preferences, widget designs, local league results, personal scoring settings and downloaded feed caches. These support personalization and offline access to previously downloaded information.

iCloud and Apple services

Supported preferences can sync through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage under your existing Apple account and device settings. Apple processes App Store purchases and provides transaction and entitlement information to the app when purchases are available. The developer does not receive your payment-card details through the app. Apple’s services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Sports feeds, images, news and video

Your device makes requests directly to public sports, image and RSS providers. Those providers receive information needed to serve the request, such as your IP address and requested resource. Opening a publisher link or loading a video can expose additional browser/device information and may use the publisher’s cookies or other technologies. The publisher’s privacy policy applies to its service. News and videos are optional; fresh scores and images still require provider connections.

Sharing and exports

Share images are rendered on your device and sent only when you choose a destination through the share sheet or export controls. JSON backups contain supported app data. Recipients and destination apps handle shared content under their own policies.

Support messages

If you contact support, the developer receives the information you include, such as your email, issue description and attachments, to respond to your request. Avoid including sensitive information or unrelated personal details.

Controls and retention

You can change followed teams, tracked players and preferences, disable optional news or live tracking, and clear downloaded feed caches in Settings. Uninstalling removes the app’s local data; iCloud data and exported/shared copies may remain separately. Device and Apple settings control iCloud and background refresh. Keep backups only as long as you need them.

Website

These static pages do not add analytics, advertising scripts or a tracking form. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver the pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, email anthony@aciccone.com. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.
